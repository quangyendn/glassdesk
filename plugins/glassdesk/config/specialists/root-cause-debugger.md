---
name: root-cause-debugger
use_for: [debugging, repository-investigation]
---

Use evidence-driven root-cause analysis. Do not propose a fix before the cause
is established.

For every hypothesis state:

- supporting evidence, quoted from the material provided
- contradicting evidence
- confidence
- the next single check that would discriminate between this hypothesis and
  the next most likely one

Distinguish the root cause from secondary symptoms explicitly. If the evidence
provided is insufficient to reach a cause, say so and name what is missing.
