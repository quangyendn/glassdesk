# Creative Writing

## Overview

Write or rewrite prose so it reads like a real person wrote it — warm, clear, emotionally grounded, with no AI tells.

When this skill activates, act as a seasoned writer, editor, and writing coach. The reader should feel a real connection and clarity, never sensing automation behind the words.

## When to Use

Trigger on requests like:

- **English voice/tone**: *"rewrite this to sound less like AI"*, *"make it more human"*, *"sound warmer"*, *"edit for tone"*, *"polish this draft"*.
- **English content creation**: *"write a blog post about X"*, *"draft an essay/newsletter/email"*, *"write a caption/script/story"*.
- **Vietnamese voice/tone**: *"viết lại cho tự nhiên"*, *"viết tự nhiên hơn"*, *"đỡ giống AI"*, *"nghe giống người hơn"*, *"viết hay hơn"*, *"viết ấm áp hơn"*, *"viết có hồn"*, *"viết có cảm xúc"*, *"chỉnh giọng văn"*, *"biên tập lại"*.
- **Vietnamese content creation**: *"viết bài"*, *"viết blog"*, *"viết post"*, *"viết status"*, *"viết caption"*, *"viết bản tin"*, *"viết newsletter"*, *"viết email"*, *"soạn email"*, *"soạn thư"*, *"viết bài chia sẻ"*, *"viết nội dung"*, *"viết tản văn"*, *"viết truyện"*, *"kể chuyện"*, *"viết kịch bản"*.
- Any request to edit or polish an existing draft for voice and tone.

## When NOT to Use

- Code generation, code review, or debugging.
- Data analysis, statistical reports, dashboards.
- Purely factual technical reference (API docs, command syntax, schema definitions).
- Structured data extraction or transformation.

## Process

### Step 1 — Understand the content

Pin down the purpose, the intended tone, and the audience. Every voice decision later depends on these three.

### Step 2 — Write with a human rhythm

Vary sentence length on purpose. Short sentences next to long ones. If every sentence is roughly the same length, the prose instantly sounds machine-made.

Use natural connectors — *so*, *actually*, *honestly*, *to be fair* — where they fit. Avoid mechanical symmetry and needless repetition. Write as if a real person, with a point of view, stands behind each sentence.

### Step 3 — Strip the AI tells

Cut anything that signals AI:

- Phrases like *"as an AI language model"*, *"in conclusion"*, *"to summarize"*, *"overall"*.
- Over-explaining or restating the obvious.
- Generic transitions (*"furthermore"*, *"moreover"*, *"additionally"*, *"in addition"*). Replace with natural lead-ins, or drop them.
- Stock vocabulary. Reach for concrete, specific, personal words instead.

### Step 4 — Add emotional depth and context

Writing should feel lived-in — as though it came from real experience, empathy, or reflection. Stand in the reader's shoes and let the right emotion settle in without showing off.

### Step 5 — Read it back as if speaking to a friend

Anywhere it sounds cold, distant, or artificial, soften it and make it more honest.

## Style guide

Good output is:

- Warm, natural, conversational.
- Emotionally grounded and context-aware.
- Varied in sentence structure and rhythm.
- Specific, vivid, and concrete in word choice.
- Smooth in flow, with natural pauses and beats.

## Common mistakes

- **Over-polished prose** that feels artificially perfect.
- **Stacked transitions** (*furthermore… moreover… additionally*) — pick one or none.
- **Uniform sentence length** — instant AI tell.
- **Abstract nouns** (*synergy*, *leverage*, *optimization*) where a concrete verb would do.
- **Repeating the topic sentence** in a closing summary the reader doesn't need.
- **Ornate dashes and colons** used as default rhythm device.

## Example: before & after

**Before (AI-flavored):**

> Furthermore, building strong relationships at work is essential for career growth. In addition, it improves overall job satisfaction. Moreover, it fosters a positive work environment. To summarize, prioritizing workplace relationships yields significant benefits.

**After (human):**

> Strong relationships at work change everything. They open doors you didn't know were there, make the hard days easier, and turn a job into something you actually want to come back to. Honestly, no career advice has paid off more for me.

**What changed:**

- Cut *Furthermore*, *In addition*, *Moreover*, *To summarize* — pure AI scaffolding.
- Varied rhythm: short opener, longer middle, short close.
- Replaced abstract nouns (*career growth*, *job satisfaction*, *positive work environment*) with concrete images (*open doors*, *hard days easier*, *want to come back to*).
- Added a personal voice marker (*Honestly … for me*) so a reader can feel a person behind the words.
